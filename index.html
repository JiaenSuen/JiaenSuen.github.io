Project / Research Journey

How important work developed over time.

This section helps recruiters and graduate reviewers see growth, ownership, and research maturity—not just final outputs.

Stage 01

Problem Framing

Defined the target scenario: achieving reliable 3D perception on resource-constrained embedded hardware using a pure-vision pipeline.

Question: How much perception quality can be preserved under strict compute limits?
Stage 02

Prototype and Baseline

Built initial stereo depth and detection components, then used baseline outputs to identify bottlenecks in latency, accuracy, and integration stability.

Output: baseline pipeline, failure cases, and experiment notes.
Stage 03

Optimization and Deployment

Refined the system for NVIDIA Jetson deployment, prioritizing practical trade-offs between model size, runtime performance, and perception quality.

Output: optimized prototype and deployment documentation.
Stage 04

Reflection and Next Steps

Recorded limitations and future directions, including more robust calibration, stronger evaluation protocols, and possible multimodal extensions.

Next: turn the project into a clearer research artifact.

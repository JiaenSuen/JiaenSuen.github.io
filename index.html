Computer Vision · Robotic Perception · Robotics AI & Learning

I study efficient intelligence for compact robotic systems, with my strongest current focus in computer vision and 3D perception. My work spans stereo geometry, embedded deep learning, and robotic perception, while I am expanding toward reinforcement learning, robot learning, and integrated perception–decision–control systems. Longer term, I am interested in compact physical and latent world models for embodied AI.

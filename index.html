01 · Research

Research interests

A compact view of the concepts connecting my current and planned work.

01

Resource-Constrained Robotic Perception

Reliable visual perception for small robotic platforms where sensing geometry, compute, memory, and latency are first-class research constraints.

02

Stereo Vision, Geometry & Domain Shift

Small-baseline stereo depth estimation across camera, scene, and sensing-domain changes, with emphasis on accuracy–efficiency trade-offs in real embedded deployment.

03

Representation Transfer & Efficient Learning

Knowledge distillation and controlled representation reuse across networks and tasks, separating genuine representation benefit from adapter capacity and optimization effects.

04

Robot Learning & Embodied Intelligence

Learning representations, memory, and corrective control for compact agents acting under partial observability, with a longer-term interest in predictive latent dynamics.

03 · Selected Projects

Selected projects

Three projects selected for research relevance rather than breadth.

Computer Vision · Retrieval · Local LLM Independent Project

E pluribus — Multi-Task Ecological Research Assistant

End-to-end ecological AI framework combining visual perception, hybrid retrieval, reranking, structured context engineering, and evidence synthesis. E-ConvNeXt reached 88.76% fine-grained classification accuracy while using substantially fewer parameters and GFLOPs than ResNet18.

Project ↗ GitHub ↗
Reinforcement Learning · Robotics Independent Project

Robotic Reinforcement Learning Sandbox

A multi-task POMDP benchmark for studying recurrence, goal switching, topological aliasing, dynamic prediction, and safety. A lightweight 4-DOF manipulation prototype using DLS-IK and residual PPO achieved 99.0% pick-and-place success over 864 surrogate evaluation episodes.

Project ↗ GitHub ↗
Metaheuristics · Combinatorial Optimization Research Study

Structure-Aware Metaheuristic Search for Coupled Procurement–Routing Optimization

A research study of how solution representation, neighborhood semantics, and adaptive control determine search topology, feasibility, and operator effectiveness in coupled procurement–routing optimization. Comparative mechanism analysis includes DWOA-AVNS and DFOX-APNS, with the strongest configuration reducing median RPD by 83.2% and reaching 1.72× GA-relative AUC efficiency.
